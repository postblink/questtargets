# Quest Targets 1.7.0

## RareScanner on WoW Forever

- **World map.** Left-click RareScanner icons to mark rares, then use the purple **RareScanner Button** to search the marked list. Hover over the button to see the list. Each click typically checks **2–4 rares**, depending on name length. Click again to check the next group.
- **Zone and dungeon search.** Under **Buttons → Targeting bar**, enable **Search all rares in the zone** to include the unmarked rares RareScanner lists for your current area, including supported dungeons. The option starts off and the list updates as you change zones.
- **Hotkeys and visibility.** Assign up to two keys under **Hotkeys → Target RareScanner rares**. The purple button is shown by default and turns gray when there are no targets to search for. It stays visible during combat and resumes searching after combat. **Show RareScanner Button** controls its visibility.
- **Minimap.** Left-click a rare’s icon to search for that rare directly. A subtle radar pulse confirms the click. If the minimap handles the first click, click again to attempt targeting. RareScanner searches work out of combat and within WoW’s targeting range.

## Targeting bar

- **Buttons → Targeting bar** groups button visibility, size, position, horizontal or vertical layout and spacing from 0 to 32 px. The default layout is horizontal with 4 px spacing.
- **Show bronze border** and **Show dividers** can be enabled independently. The bronze frame surrounds the entire bar. The gold-to-silver dividers have small stars at their ends and rotate with a vertical bar.
- **Modern quest compass.** The classic text button switches to the compass at its saved position. Adjust its size with **Button size**.

## Patch Notes window

- Patch notes open once per account on your first login with a new version and stay open until you close them. Reopen them from **Options → AddOns → Quest Targets → Patch Notes**. They use the addon’s selected language and group changes by feature.

One download supports Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, WoW Forever and Retail. RareScanner integration requires RareScanner on WoW Forever. Install QuestieDB separately on the supported Classic clients and Forever.
