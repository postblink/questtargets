# Quest Targets 1.7.2

## RareScanner on WoW Forever

- **Check nearby rares first.** The purple button and its hotkey check marked rares or the current zone list in order of distance to the nearest known RareScanner spawn point.

## Targeting bar

- **Use your current position on every click.** Out of combat, buttons that search by distance read your position when you click or press a hotkey. If you've moved, the search starts at the top of the updated list. While you stay in place, repeated clicks check the next names.

One download supports Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, WoW Forever and Retail. RareScanner integration requires RareScanner on WoW Forever. Install QuestieDB separately on the supported Classic clients and Forever.
