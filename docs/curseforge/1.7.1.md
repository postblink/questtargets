# Quest Targets 1.7.1

## RareScanner on WoW Forever

- **Keep your target.** When a living rare from your search list is selected, clicking the purple button again or pressing its hotkey keeps that target selected. Clear the target or wait until it dies to continue searching.

## Targeting bar

- **Separate target searches.** The red master button looks for quest targets plus targets from the active RestedXP guide step when that integration is enabled. The blue RestedXP compass searches only the active guide step and cycles independently of the red button. The yellow ItemTree and purple RareScanner buttons each search their own lists.

One download supports Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, WoW Forever and Retail. RareScanner integration requires RareScanner on WoW Forever. Install QuestieDB separately on the supported Classic clients and Forever.
