# Quest Targets 1.6.1

- **Bind your mouse buttons from anywhere.** Click a hotkey field, then press a middle or extra mouse button, or scroll the wheel, wherever your pointer is in the game window. Modifier combinations and confirmation before replacing an occupied binding are supported.
- **EllesmereUI quest buttons.** On Forever, EllesmereUI's Custom Quest Tracker can now show a target button beside each quest with known targets. Left-click to find a quest target or right-click to select your focused quest. Enable the buttons under **Options → AddOns → Quest Targets → Buttons**. They hide during combat and return afterwards.
- **More room to position tracker buttons.** Questie and EllesmereUI each have their own spacing control from **−240 to +240 px**. Button tooltips, the minimap button and tracker button options are now grouped under **Buttons**.
- **Optional quieter targeting.** Enable **Mute target-clearing sound** to suppress the sound played when Quest Targets clears a target, including during repeated searches. WoW's target-selection sound remains audible, even if a search briefly selects a dead mob. This option cannot selectively suppress that selection sound for dead matches, because a name search can only confirm whether its match is dead after selecting it.
- **Easier feedback.** Scan the QR code in the feedback window to open the feedback page on another device.

One download supports Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Forever and Retail. EllesmereUI tracker buttons require its supported Custom Quest Tracker on Forever. QuestieDB and supported third-party addons are separate downloads.
